How the 15% Oil Limit Works

In this class, you can use up to 15% liquid oils in your body butter formula. The rest is butters, base ingredients, and fragrance.

  • Think of 15% as your “oil budget”.
  • Every oil you pick spends a little of that budget.
  • Use your worksheet to add up your chosen percentages until you reach (or stay under) 15%.
Tips While You Create
  • Start with 2–3 oils instead of many — simple formulas are easier to repeat.
  • Circle your favorite oils in the Oil Finder, then plug them into your worksheet.
  • Notice how different textures feel: some are light and dry-touch, others rich and cushiony.
  • Your blend should feel like you — there is no one “perfect” recipe.
